# Project Components

## Modules represented in the database
1. Project and site management
2. Contractor and work-package management
3. Material and supplier management
4. Purchase orders and purchase-order items
5. Delivery and delivery items
6. Site stock and material issues
7. Labour teams and progress entries
8. Contractor bills and payments
9. Material balance and outstanding payment reports

## Relationship summary
- One project can contain many sites.
- One site can contain many work packages and purchase orders.
- A supplier can be linked to many purchase orders.
- A purchase order can contain many items and deliveries.
- A site can stock multiple materials.
- A work package can have multiple progress entries and contractor bills.
- A contractor bill can have multiple payments.
